Bring your
music.

Moving apps should not mean starting over. Paste a link to a playlist or an album and Lull finds every track in its catalogue, then saves it as your own playlist. Your favourite artists come along too.

  1. Copy the linkOpen a playlist or album in your old app and tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Paste it into LullCollection, then Import. Lull reads the list and matches each song.
  3. Keep listeningSave it as a playlist, add the songs to your likes, follow the artists.
